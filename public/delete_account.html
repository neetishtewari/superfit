Account & Data Deletion

At Superfit, we believe you own your data. If you decide to stop using our app, we provide options to delete your account and all associated physical, health, and fitness metrics.

How to Delete Your Data

You can request account and data deletion through either of the two methods below:

Method 1: Immediate In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account and clear all local and cloud data instantly within the Superfit mobile application:

  • Open the Superfit app and navigate to the Settings screen.
  • Tap Logout (Clear Cloud Data).
  • This immediately deletes your entire cloud profile, synced calories, macro targets, workouts, steps, and sleep telemetry from our Firestore servers, wipes the local SQLite storage on your device, and signs you out.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app and want to request deletion of your synced data directly from the web, click the button below to send a request. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 business days.

Please make sure to send the request from the email address registered with your Superfit account.

Request Deletion via Email

What Data is Deleted?

Upon processing your request, all of the following records associated with your account are permanently erased from our systems:

  • Personal Info: Registered email address, unique Firebase User ID, age, and gender.
  • Health Metrics: Height and weight history.
  • Fitness & Telemetry: Logged workouts, steps, active calories burned, sleep quality metrics, and nutrition logs.

Note: Account deletion is permanent. Once deleted, your telemetry history and custom metabolic target profiles cannot be recovered.
