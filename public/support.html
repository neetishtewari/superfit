Quick answers

How do I log a meal?

On Today, tap Snap your plate to log from a photo, or the mic to say what you ate. You can also type it, scan a barcode or a nutrition label, or pick something you've eaten before.

Steps, workouts and sleep aren't showing

Superfit reads them from Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android. Open Settings in Superfit and connect it. On iPhone you can also check Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Superfit and turn the categories on.

The mic doesn't work

Superfit needs the microphone, and on iPhone also Speech Recognition. Turn them on in your phone's Settings > Superfit.

Moving to a new phone

Sign in with the same account and your meals, workouts and progress come back from your backup.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings > Delete account. See the account deletion page for other ways and for what gets deleted.
