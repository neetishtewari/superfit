What is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is the total number of calories your body burns in a single day.

It is calculated by taking your BMR (calories burned at rest) and multiplying it by your Baseline Activity Level.

This serves as the baseline budget from which we subtract/add calories depending on your fitness goals.
